Place your high-quality GLTF/GLB models for the player, NPCs, trees, and props in this folder.

Recommended filenames:
- player.glb
- npc.glb
- tree.glb
- bench.glb
- lamp.glb
- car.glb
- mailbox.glb

Models should be rigged and include animations (idle, walk, run, interact, etc) where appropriate.

You can source free models from sites like Sketchfab, Quaternius, or Poly Pizza, or create your own in Blender and export as GLB/GLTF.
